Eco Ardence is one of the most uniform housing developments in Shah Alam — which makes renovation planning easier than most townships. The basic floor plate, structural grid, and electrical capacity is the same across most blocks.

## The standard layout

Most Eco Ardence link houses share:

- 22'x70' footprint (typical)
- Open-plan ground floor with structural columns at predictable points
- Single-flight staircase on the side wall
- Standard 5-bedroom, 4-bathroom upstairs configuration
- Rear yard with built-in laundry / wet kitchen rough-in

Knowing the structural grid in advance saves design time and money.

## Common renovation scopes

The three most common Eco Ardence renovations:

1. **Wet + dry kitchen split** — moving the cooker hob outside to the rear yard
2. **Master bedroom upgrade** — opening into the adjacent bedroom for a walk-in wardrobe
3. **Ground-floor study / formal area** — converting the standard powder room corner

## Township-specific rules to know

- **No facade changes** without architectural committee approval
- **Noise hours** restricted to 9am–5pm Mon–Sat
- **Material delivery** through township gate logistics
- **Defect period** with developer is separate from your renovation contractor's defect period — don't confuse them

## Cost ranges

For mid-2026 Eco Ardence renovations:

- Light refresh: **RM80k–RM130k**
- Moderate: **RM160k–RM280k**
- Full transformation: **RM350k+**

Pricing tends to run slightly higher than older Shah Alam townships because access logistics add labour cost.

## What we'd plan for

- Get the as-built drawings from the developer **before** you brief any designer
- Confirm structural columns and slab thickness with a quick site survey
- Submit any facade-touching design to the township early — approvals take time
- Budget 10–15% contingency for hidden electrical or plumbing variations
