We ran the same renovation scope past four PJ contractors and four Damansara contractors. Here's what came back.

## The test scope

A 2,200 sqft double-storey corner unit. Full kitchen, two bathrooms, wiring upgrade, ceiling, flooring throughout, built-in TV console + master wardrobe, exterior repaint.

## What the quotes looked like

| Area | Lowest quote | Highest quote | Median |
|---|---|---|---|
| PJ | RM168,000 | RM245,000 | RM198,000 |
| Damansara | RM192,000 | RM298,000 | RM228,000 |

A 15% premium for Damansara on the median. Nothing in the scope justified the gap — same materials, same finish-grade, same warranties.

## Where the quality difference actually shows up

The difference between firms within each area was bigger than the difference between areas.

- **Best of PJ:** decades-old family firms with in-house carpentry. Less polished proposals, faster site work, fewer surprises.
- **Best of Damansara:** design-led studios with strong 3D visualisation, stronger material curation, slightly slower delivery.
- **Worst of both:** lump-sum quotes, vague timelines, refusal to walk an active site.

## Project management

PJ contractors lean toward a hands-on PIC who's on-site 3+ days a week. Damansara studios more often use a project manager managing multiple sites — fine if they're disciplined, painful if they're not.

## How to choose between the two

- If your priority is **cost + reliability**: lean PJ family firm.
- If your priority is **design + finish detailing**: lean Damansara studio.
- If your priority is **single-team accountability** for both design and build: pick a design-and-build firm regardless of area.

The "best" contractor is rarely the most expensive one. It's the one whose strengths match the part of your project most likely to go wrong.
